# Code of conduct

Treeish welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. Participate respectfully, constructively, and in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Focus criticism on technical decisions and observable behavior.
- Be patient with questions and provide actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person’s private information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and correct course when harm occurs.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption,
and personal attacks are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an
appropriate private GitHub channel. Reports will be reviewed with attention to
the safety and privacy of everyone involved. Maintainers may edit or remove
contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
necessary.
